# Offer

## What we sell
We get a used-car dealership's inventory online and sold faster - photography, listing, and merchandising handled end to end.

## Who we sell to
- Titles: Dealer Principal / Owner, General Manager, Used-Car Manager
- Company stage: independent and small-group dealerships (not OEM franchise corporate)
- Size: 1-8 rooftops, roughly 30-400 units in stock
- Vertical: licensed motor-vehicle dealers (the ones on the provincial dealer registry)

## The problem we solve
Used inventory sits on the lot for weeks with three phone photos and no description while it depreciates. The owner knows online merchandising sells cars faster but the sales team never has time to do it well. We take reconditioned units to a full online listing - photos, write-ups, syndication - within a day of them landing.

## Trigger event
A dealership that just got licensed, opened or expanded a lot, or hired a used-car manager - the moment used-vehicle volume becomes someone's explicit job.

## Most common objection
"My salespeople already photograph the cars." (Reality: they shoot three blurry photos between customers; aged inventory is the proof it isn't working.)

A generic archetype: an independent dealer averaging 45 days-to-sale cut it to under 30 after moving photography and listing off the sales floor. Replace with your own named dealer and number before outbound.
