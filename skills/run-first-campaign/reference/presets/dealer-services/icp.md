# ICP

## Engagement signals
What makes a dealership a great fit. 3-7 bullets.
- Aged used inventory - units listed 45+ days, or listings with one or two photos
- Recently licensed or newly expanded rooftop on the provincial dealer registry
- Hiring a used-car manager, lot attendant, or "reconditioning" role
- Active on a marketplace (AutoTrader-style) but with thin, inconsistent listings
- Owner-operated, hands-on with how cars get merchandised

## Anti-signals
What makes you walk away. 3-5 bullets.
- OEM franchise locked into a corporate-mandated photography/listing vendor
- One- or two-car hobby lot with no real inventory turn
- Wholesale-only or auction reseller that never retails to consumers
- Wants a guaranteed sales lift before any pilot
- No online presence at all and no intent to build one

## Disqualifying conditions
Hard exclusions. The deal does not happen if any of these are true.
- Not a licensed dealer (curbstoner / unlicensed reseller)
- Zero used retail inventory (new-only or parts-only)
- No budget authority in the room

## Buying committee
Who actually signs off + who blocks.
- Decision maker: Dealer Principal / Owner
- Champion: Used-Car Manager or GM who owns turn time
- Blocker: an incumbent marketing or photography vendor already on retainer
