# Offer

## What we sell
We get local home-services contractors more booked jobs by modernizing how customers reach them - online booking, fast follow-up, and review capture.

## Who we sell to
- Titles: Owner / Operator, Office Manager, Service Manager
- Company stage: established local contractors, past owner-only, not a national franchise
- Size: 5-50 field techs, one or a few trucks-to-crews
- Vertical: licensed trades - HVAC, plumbing, electrical (the ones on the provincial trade-licensing registry or a trade-association directory)

## The problem we solve
The phone is the whole booking system: a customer who calls after hours gets voicemail and books the next contractor instead. Estimates go out and never get chased. The owner is on a truck all day and can't run the office. We put online booking and automated follow-up in place so no job leaks while the crew is in the field.

## Trigger event
A contractor hiring field techs (capacity to fill), heading into a seasonal demand ramp, or running an aging website with no online booking.

## Most common objection
"We get all our work from referrals and Google already." (Reality: referrals plateau, and the missed after-hours calls never show up as a number they see.)

## Proof
A generic archetype: a plumbing contractor adding online booking recovered a dozen after-hours jobs a month that had been going to voicemail. Replace with your own named contractor and number before outbound.
