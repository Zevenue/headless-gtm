# Qualification Brief - template

The brief is the compiled ruleset for one client. It is shown for approval
before any judging, saved to the client config, and reused on later runs.
Calibration corrections amend it in place.

```markdown
# Qualification Brief - {Client Name}

**Client:** {domain} - {one sentence: what they sell, to whom, what pain it removes}
**Prepared from:** {profile | intake | website research (pages read)}
**Calibrated with:** {model or "in-session"} · **Status:** {draft | approved | calibrated}

## Check 1 - Business nature (primary)
Qualified prospects look like: {2-3 sentences describing the kind of business
that buys}
Competitor definition (auto-DQ): {the client's own product category, stated
plainly, with example vendor names if known}

## Check 2 - Firmographics
- Headcount band: {min}–{max} (uncertain zone: {floor/2}–{min} and {max}–{max×2})
- Geographies: {list}
- Industries / segments: {list or "any within business-nature check"}

## Check 3 - Independence & liveness (universal, always on)
Standard catalog rules apply: acquired, merged, dormant, shell → disqualified
with evidence.

## Dynamic checks (this client specifically)
1. {check} - {why it matters for this client; what evidence satisfies it}
2. {check} - {...}

## Exclusions
{named accounts: existing customers, partners, or "none provided"}

## Client-specific rules learned in calibration
- {rule added from a correction, e.g. "hybrid manufacturers with named
  product lines qualify"}
```

Keep the brief under a page. It is the single source of truth for verdicts;
if a judgment can't be traced to a line in the brief or the DQ catalog, the
verdict is `uncertain`.
